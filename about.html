Tajhiz Kalaye Mabna is an Iranian industrial equipment supply and trading company established in 2009, providing specialized procurement, sourcing, import, export and supply solutions for industrial projects.

Our approach begins with understanding the technical, commercial and project requirements behind each procurement need. We identify suitable equipment, products and supply sources, then coordinate the procurement process to support reliable and well-aligned industrial supply.

Our scope includes piping equipment and fittings , industrial equipment and components, and specialized equipment sourcing for industries including oil and gas, petrochemical, refinery, power generation, chemical, mining, cement, food and other industrial facilities.
