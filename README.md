## ✦ About TKMabna

**Tajhiz Kalaye Mabna (TKMabna)** is an Iranian industrial equipment supply and trading company established in **2009 (1388)**, providing specialized procurement, sourcing, supply, import, export, and commercial solutions for industrial projects and organizations.

With a focus on **industrial equipment, piping equipment, engineering products, procurement, and international sourcing**, TKMabna works to connect industrial requirements with reliable products, qualified suppliers, and efficient supply solutions.

Our approach combines:

- Industrial expertise
- Strategic procurement
- Reliable sourcing
- International trade
- Technical understanding
- Quality-focused supply
- Responsive customer support

> **From Requirement to Reliable Supply.**

## ✦ What We Do

TKMabna provides a range of industrial and commercial services designed to support procurement requirements from initial inquiry through final supply.

### Industrial Equipment Supply

Supply of industrial equipment and components for a wide range of applications, projects, and operating environments.

### Piping Equipment & Fittings

Sourcing and supply of piping products, fittings, valves, flanges, pipes, and related components for industrial applications.

### Procurement & Sourcing

Identification, evaluation, and sourcing of products from qualified manufacturers, suppliers, and international markets.

### Import & Export

Commercial and international trade services supporting the import and export of permitted industrial and commercial goods.

### Technical Support

Technical assistance in product identification, specification review, equipment selection, and procurement requirements.

### Project Supply

Supporting industrial projects with coordinated procurement and equipment supply solutions according to project requirements.
