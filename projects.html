Project Supply Workflow.

From Requirement to Delivery

Every industrial project starts with a specific requirement. Our project supply workflow connects technical evaluation, equipment sourcing, procurement coordination, and delivery planning through a structured approach designed around the needs of each project.

Whether the requirement involves industrial equipment, piping components, or specialized supply needs, the process is shaped by technical specifications, project priorities, and applicable commercial requirements.

The Process Architecture

A clear path through complex requirements.

Requirement-Led Approach
  1. STEP / 01

    Requirement Review

    Review the project scope, equipment requirements, technical documentation, quantities, and requested delivery conditions.

    Understand
  2. STEP / 02

    Technical Evaluation

    Clarify specifications, material requirements, applicable standards, and compatibility with the intended industrial application.

    Define
  3. STEP / 03

    Sourcing & Supplier Coordination

    Identify suitable sourcing options and coordinate supplier information against the project's technical and commercial requirements.

    Source
  4. STEP / 04

    Procurement Alignment

    Align quotations, scope, lead times, documentation, and commercial conditions before moving forward with the agreed supply arrangement.

    Coordinate
  5. STEP / 05

    Supply & Follow-Through

    Coordinate supply arrangements, delivery-related communication, and available documentation in line with the agreed project requirements.

    Deliver

Each workflow is adapted to the scope, technical complexity, sourcing conditions, and delivery requirements of the individual project.

Technical & Quality Approach

REQUIREMENTS BEFORE PROCUREMENT

A MORE CONSIDERED WAY TO SOURCE

Technical Clarity. Quality by Design.

Reliable industrial equipment procurement starts with a clear understanding of technical requirements. Tajhiz Kalaye Mabna approaches project supply by aligning equipment specifications, applicable quality criteria and procurement documentation before sourcing decisions are finalized.

This structured approach helps identify information gaps, clarify supply expectations and support informed coordination between project requirements and proposed equipment.

THE TECHNICAL REVIEW FRAMEWORK

From requirements to supply readiness.

A requirement-led sequence for reviewing technical information and coordinating procurement decisions.

STAGE / 01

Requirement Analysis

Review equipment specifications, datasheets, technical schedules and project documentation to establish a clear understanding of the requested supply scope.

INPUT / REQUIREMENTS

STAGE / 02

Technical Alignment

Clarify material grades, dimensions, ratings, operating conditions and relevant technical standards against the information provided for the project.

REVIEW / SPECIFICATIONS

STAGE / 03

Quality Documentation

Identify applicable inspection records, material certificates, conformity documents and traceability requirements, subject to the project specification and agreed scope.

DOCUMENTS / QUALITY

STAGE / 04

Procurement Coordination

Coordinate clarified requirements, supplier information, delivery expectations and agreed documentation needs to support a more controlled procurement process.

OUTPUT / SUPPLY READINESS
